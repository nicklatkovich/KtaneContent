On the Subject of Mazes BiMazes

Uhhh. Are you sure this maze is solvable? It feels like someone added too many extra walls.

This seems to be some kind of maze, probably stolen off of a restaurant placemat.

  • Find the maze two mazes with matching circular markings.
  • The defuser must navigate the white light to the red triangle using the arrow buttons.
  • Warning: Do not cross the lines shown in the maze. These lines are invisible on the bomb.
  • If the triangle is red, defuser is in a maze indicated by the first marking
    (in reading order).
  • If the triangle is green, defuser is in a maze indicated by the second marking.
  • When an arrow button is pressed, the defuser moves in the indicated direction and then switches to the other maze.
  • If an arrow button is held down for one second, the defuser moves without switching mazes.
